# Meowzix v0.8

Download **Meowzix-v0.8.apk** for Android 8.0 or newer. Verify the signed APK using **SHA256SUMS.txt**.

## What's new
- **Library:** Increased card spacing and 70%-transparent track, artist and album surfaces; visible-only artwork loading.
- **Launcher:** Peach background, safe-size cat logo and non-inverted monochrome themed icon.
- **Telegram forwarding:** Pinned title, options, search and folders in both player and notification overlay; faster folder loading and smoother dismissal.
- **Seekbars:** Live equalizer / Android wave choice with current-track previews; Settings scrubbing does not affect actual playback and FFT stops offscreen.
- **Player and lyrics:** Improved gestures, lyric source management, track-based accents, accessible action menus, configurable sleep timer and fade.
- **Vinyl widget:** Animated spinning record with playback gesture controls.
- **Live Update:** Optional notification controls, song progress and track metadata, with configurable side actions.
- **Home and playlists:** Refreshed translucent UI, compact layout, sticky headers and smoother transitions.
- **Telegram integration:** Improved chat sync, playlist metadata, sending/retry behavior and navigation.
- **Playback and efficiency:** Android Auto browsing, audio focus/resume refinements and lower overhead for artwork/visualization.
- **Reliability:** Additional tests, migrations and stability fixes.
