# Meowzix 0.7

A more personal player, a cleaner settings experience, and easier Telegram forwarding.

These release notes cover changes since **v0.6.5**.

## What's new

- **Animated vinyl player:** Turn the current cover art into a spinning record, complete with a gramophone tonearm that retracts when playback pauses.
- **Redesigned Settings:** A clearer hierarchy of grouped glassmorphism panels for appearance, playback, recommendations, Telegram, downloads, privacy, backup, and advanced controls. Destructive actions have their own confirmation area.
- **Personal color themes:** Choose a preset accent or enter a custom HEX color, independently of Light/Dark/System mode. On Android 12+, you can follow the wallpaper-based system palette. Tonal text and surface colors are generated with readability in mind. Supported Android 13+ launchers can also recolor the monochrome app icon using Themed Icons.
- **Telegram chat folders in Forward:** Browse your Telegram folders to find recipients quickly, then select the actual chat to receive the track. Available both in-app and from the notification flow.
- **Long titles that scroll:** Overflowing song names move to reveal the full title, right-to-left for English and left-to-right for Persian. Reduced Motion keeps them static.
- **Artist shortcuts:** Tap the artist name in Now Playing to jump to that artist in your Library.

## Improvements and fixes

- Improved the notification's **Forward** handoff, including preservation of the selected track, a bottom-anchored Telegram picker, and clearer destination text. The final Quick Settings dismissal behavior is controlled by Android/SystemUI and may vary by device.
- Improved recovery after another app temporarily interrupts audio focus, including an active crossfade; intentional pauses remain pauses.
- Removed an unintended extra background/shadow around the Library track-actions popup.
- Fixed additional Forward overlay sizing and appearance issues.

## Changed

- Removed the separate rule-based **Smart Playlists** screen and runtime. Existing legacy data and backup compatibility are retained. Regular playlists and **Smart Shuffle** are unaffected.
